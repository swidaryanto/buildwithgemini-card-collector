# My agent: card-collector
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps collectors discover, trade, and battle with a catalog of collectible creature cards.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Collector's inventory/deck, trade history, battle record, and favorite card attributes
- Tools: Card lookup, pack opening, trade execution, and deck management
- Catalog/UI: Collectible card collection rendered as rich cards and comparison tables
- Image gen: Custom creature artwork for newly discovered cards and special holo editions
- Sandbox: Battle stat calculations, damage simulation, and deck power rating computation

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for battle calculations, Cloud Trace for tracing tool latency
